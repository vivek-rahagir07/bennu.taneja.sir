Biographical Insight

Beenu Kumar Taneja is a highly respected corporate trainer, entrepreneur, and leadership development expert who has made a powerful impact in the field of professional training and organisational transformation in India. With over 19 years of professional experience, including 17 years dedicated to training and development, he has established himself as a thought leader in leadership development, behavioural skills, and performance enhancement across diverse industries.

Known for his practical, empathetic, and insight-driven approach, Beenu focuses on transforming individuals and teams through engaging learning experiences that go far beyond traditional lecture-based training. In 2012, he founded Getting Roots Coaching & Training Pvt. Ltd., a dynamic platform that delivers customised training solutions designed to unlock human potential and drive organisational growth.

Based in Delhi, India, and born on 29 June 1985, Beenu continues to strengthen his academic foundation by pursuing a Ph.D., MBA, and BBA from Amity University while inspiring professionals and organisations to achieve meaningful and lasting success.
